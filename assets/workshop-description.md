Employees get better at their job by doing it.

In the base game, an employee hired at 19% stays at 19% unless you pay for training or assign an HR manager. With Learn on the Job, every hour an employee spends on shift nudges their primary skill upward.

**Growth**

- Below 50%: +2 points per step
- 50% to 70%: +1 point per step
- 70% and up: no on-the-job growth. Formal training is still the way past "average".

A step is a number of on-shift hours, set by a slider under **Options > Mods** (default 40, one full-time week). At the default, a full-time 19% hire reaches 50% after about 15 and a half weeks on shift, and 70% after another 20.

**Details**

- Only counts hours when the employee has a shift, isn't in training, and isn't off sick.
- Only the primary skill (the one shown next to their name) grows. Skills are never lowered.
- Progress is saved in your save file. The mod stores nothing of its own.
- Wages don't rise with on-the-job growth. Formal training still raises wages as usual.
- An employee who crosses 50% becomes eligible for an extra job demand, same as a trained employee.
- Works during sleep, fast-forward, and the time machine.
- Roles that are never scheduled on a shift may not grow. Delivery drivers and headquarters managers might be affected; this hasn't been confirmed in-game yet.

**Installing and removing**

Subscribe and load a save. Loading a save with mods creates a modded copy; your original save is untouched.
